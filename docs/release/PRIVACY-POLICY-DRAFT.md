# Privacy policy (DRAFT, not legal advice)

**DRAFT for the owner and a lawyer to review before publishing.** Written against the Digital Personal Data
Protection Act, 2023. Placeholders are in angle brackets. A Malayalam version is needed before publishing.

**`<Business name>`** ("we") delivers drinking water cans in `<area>`, Kerala. This policy explains what we
collect in the WtrDrop app and admin portal, why, and your choices. Last updated: `<date>`.

## What we collect

- **Phone number**: to sign you in with an SMS code and to contact you about a delivery.
- **Name and delivery addresses** (with a landmark and, only if you tap "Save my current location", your
  location at that moment): to deliver your order.
- **Orders, deliveries, cans held, deposits and payments**: to deliver, keep your statement and keep our accounts.
- **Complaints and messages**: to answer problems you report.
- **Phone notification token** (only if you allow notifications): to tell you when an order is on the way.
- **For delivery staff**: the deliveries, cash and cans they record, and the phone their PIN is set on.

We do not collect contacts, photos or your location in the background. We do not sell or share your data for
advertising.

## Who sees it

- Our office staff and delivery staff, only what they need (for example, a driver sees the address and phone for
  their stops that day).
- Service providers that run the service for us, under contract: our hosting provider in India, MSG91 (SMS codes),
  Expo, Google and Apple (notifications only).
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

## Where and how long

Your data is stored in India. We keep it while you are our customer. When you delete your account, your name,
phone number, addresses, complaint notes and messages are erased at once. Orders, payments and the record of
cans and deposits are kept without your name or phone number for as long as tax and accounting law requires
(`<8 years>`), then deleted. Backups roll over within `<30>` days.

## Your rights

You can see your details, orders and statement in the app, correct your name and addresses, and **delete your
account** in the app (Account → Delete my account). If cans, a deposit or money are still outstanding, we settle
them with you first. You can withdraw consent to notifications in your phone's settings at any time.

For anything else, or a complaint, contact our grievance officer: `<name>`, `<phone>`, `<email>`. If you are not
satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit; sign-in codes, PINs and passwords are stored only as hashes; office actions are
logged. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Board as the law requires.

## Children

The app is for adults who order for their household or business.
